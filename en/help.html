Help

Answers to the most common questions. The full guide is in the app under Settings → Instructions.

Connecting

My PC doesn't show up in the app

Check that the phone and the PC are on the same Wi-Fi network and that MouseVortex is open on the PC (the icon in the tray, next to the clock).

The first time, Windows asks about the firewall: you need to allow private networks. If it still doesn't show up, use Connect by IP and type the IP shown in the MouseVortex window on the PC.

Can I use it without Wi-Fi?

Yes, over Bluetooth. First pair the phone with the PC in the Bluetooth settings (just once). Then the PC shows up under Over Bluetooth in the app: tap it and pair it with the PIN.

“The PC's identity changed”

It happens if you reinstalled MouseVortex on the PC. In the app, forget that PC (⋮ menu → Forget) and pair it again. If you didn't reinstall anything, don't pair it: another device might be pretending to be your PC.

Does it stay connected if I minimize the app?

Yes. While you're connected, the connection stays up even if you minimize the app or lock the phone; the “Connected to…” notification shows it and has a button to disconnect. If it drops (for example, because you walked away), the app reconnects on its own when you open it again.

How do I remove a phone from my PC?

In the MouseVortex window on the PC, tap Revoke next to the phone. If it was connected, it disconnects right away and can't get back in without pairing again.

Using it

It doesn't respond in some windows

Windows doesn't allow controlling programs opened as administrator, unless MouseVortex is also opened as administrator.

I can't send Ctrl + Alt + Del or type the Windows password

Windows doesn't allow any program to send that combination or to type on the lock screen. If you just want the screen hidden, use Screen in the More menu: the monitor turns off and comes back when you move the mouse, no password needed.

What's playing doesn't show up

The playing app has to show up in Windows' media panel (the one that appears when you change the volume). Chrome and Edge show only one tab at a time: the last one that started playing.

A video shows up black in the screenshot

Some videos (Netflix and similar) are protected and Windows doesn't allow capturing them.

My monitor doesn't show up under Brightness

Look for the “DDC/CI” option in the monitor's menu (using its buttons) and turn it on. Some monitors, TVs and adapters don't support it.

Pro and purchases

What does Pro include?

Special keys, shortcuts and the full keyboard; music, volume and mixer; presentation mode; browser; power, programs, windows, status, screenshots and brightness; clipboard with history; and several paired PCs. The free version includes the full touchpad and typing with the phone keyboard, forever.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app

Pro stays on your Google account. Sign in with the same account, open Settings → MouseVortex Pro and tap Restore purchase.

I have a promo code

Redeem it in the Play Store: your profile menu → Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code. Then, in the app, tap Restore purchase.
