Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what data the MouseVortex Android app (the “app”) and the MouseVortex PC program (the “program”) use.

In short: we don't collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, usage analytics or servers of ours. Everything MouseVortex does happens directly between your phone and your PC, over your local network or Bluetooth, and it travels encrypted.

Data we don't collect

MouseVortex doesn't send information to us or to third parties. We don't use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. We don't know who uses the app or how they use it.

What travels between your phone and your PC

To work, the app and the program exchange data only with each other, within your local network or over Bluetooth:

  • What you do in the app: cursor movements, clicks, keys and the text you type, to reproduce them on the PC.
  • Your phone's name (for example, its model), to show it in the PC's list of paired phones.
  • Information you request from the PC: the song that's playing, the volume, the list of programs and windows, processor and memory usage, the battery and screenshots.
  • The clipboard: only when you ask for it or if you turn on sync. Passwords copied from password managers are never sent.

This communication is encrypted (TLS and ChaCha20-Poly1305) and only possible between devices you paired with a QR code or a PIN.

What's stored on your devices

On the phone: the paired PCs (name, address and the key used to recognize them), your settings, shortcuts, bookmarks and favorite programs, the clipboard history and whether you bought Pro. Screenshots are saved to your gallery only if you tap Save.

On the PC: the paired phones (their name and an irreversible fingerprint, or hash, of their access key), the PC's certificate and the program's settings.

That data never leaves your devices. To delete it: in the app, forget the PC or uninstall the app; on the PC, revoke the phone or uninstall the program.

App permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code. Images are not saved or sent.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to connect to your PC over Bluetooth, if you paired it.
  • Network and Wi-Fi: to find your PC on the local network and connect to it.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the connection to the PC while the app is minimized, with a “Connected to…” notification that has a button to disconnect.
  • Vibration and keeping the screen on: in presentation mode.

Purchases

MouseVortex Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment: we don't receive your payment details, only confirmation that the purchase exists, to turn on Pro. Google handles that data according to its privacy policy.

Internet connections

The app only connects to the internet to talk to Google Play (purchases). The PC program doesn't connect to the internet, except on Linux to download the cover art of the song that's playing when the music player provides it as a web address. When you open a site from the Web menu, your PC's browser opens it, just as if you did it yourself.

Children

MouseVortex is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version on this page with its update date.
